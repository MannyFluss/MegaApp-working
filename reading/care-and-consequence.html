Read the evidence

Primary sources establish what was said or announced. They do not establish universal emotional effects. This guide credits Apple and LoveFrom teams; it does not assign every product decision to one person.

How this guide was made

Questions → primary evidence → competing interpretations → controllable prototypes → critique → verification. Each lesson keeps intention separate from interpretation. Experiments use invented interfaces and qualitative tradeoffs; their outputs are not measured usability results.

Research and method logs accompany the portable source. No private laptop or other assistant material is included.
